Vidimus attestation verifier

Verifies a Vidimus batch attestation (vidimus.attestation.v1) or a per-verdict attestation exported by the conformance app (vidimus-svc.verdict-attestation.v1). Runs entirely in this page: no server, no network, nothing leaves your browser. It reports what was checked and, just as clearly, what was not checked.
